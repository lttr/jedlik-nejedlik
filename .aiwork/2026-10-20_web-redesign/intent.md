# Intent

The marketer recorded a video walkthrough of the current site and of a
prototype they built with Claude. Their words, condensed from the
transcript:

> The site is technically great and fast, but it needs a UX clean-up. The
> homepage is where every social post and every "check out Jedlík-nejedlík"
> sends people, and it introduces only one block, „Výživa a výchova
> v propojení". It shows no face, no services, no products (free or paid),
> no events, no reviews, no media.
>
> A visitor should not have to think or search. Lead them: who we are, what
> is new right now, why to trust us, then a fork into what they need.
>
> Where do I find the e-book we are selling? Under „Pro rodiče"? Is it for
> a jedlík or a nejedlík? The subpages are long and expert-written, not
> made for a parent on maternity leave with no time.
>
> Each season has one key offer (e-book, webinar, course, consultation). It
> belongs in a highlighted menu item and a top notification bar, visible
> wherever you are on the site.
>
> A product page should say in one headline, five bullets and a price what
> it is, and then only persuade: situations you know, what changes, a short
> video of Zdeňka, media, reviews, bonus, FAQ, a free taster.
>
> Collect contacts everywhere: a pop-up with a free „desatero", the
> newsletter. Contacts feed mailing and ad targeting.
>
> Either we extract the prototype from Claude and deploy it with minimal
> work, or you use it as a guide for gradual changes over the coming weeks
> and months. Nothing in it is final.
>
> Technical: the e-book sells through SimpleShop. Put the same Meta Pixel
> into SimpleShop so we see the whole funnel (ad spend → visits → clicked
> „Chci e-book" → bought), not just the click.

Not yet decided by the association (Zdeňka and team): whether to adopt the
prototype, which parts, and in what order.
